*Revised Snippet for Zaroff's Entrance:*
    "Then a voice came from the shadows beyond the servant. It was a cultivated voice, soft and urbane, carrying an accent that was neither wholly Russian nor American. 'I am very sorry, but do not be alarmed.'
    A man stepped around the edge of the door. He was slender, wonderfully erect, dressed in immaculate evening clothes. His face was high-cheekboned, his hair was white and straight, and he smiled.
    'I am General Zaroff,' he said. His gaze was calm, appraising, respectful. 'I know you. You are Sanger Rainsford. I have read your book — *The Snow Leopards of Tibet*. I was not sure my island would ever have the pleasure.' He did not ask how Rainsford arrived. It was as if he had been expecting the interruption.
    'Your man...' Rainsford started.
    Zaroff made a simple, silent gesture of dismissal. A flick of the fingers. The giant, Ivan, immediately stepped back, the revolver lowering. He shut the great door. The echo of the lock engaging rolled through the hall like a stone falling into a deep well."
